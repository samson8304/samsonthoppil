What I do, the value I bring and what drives me.

I'm Samson Thoppil, a Business Analyst who turns data into decisions. I combine a strong business foundation with expert skills in AI, Excel and Power BI to help teams see clearly, move faster and grow with confidence.

I work across the full journey from question to answer: gathering and organising data, analysing markets and customers, building models and dashboards, and presenting findings in a way people can act on.
